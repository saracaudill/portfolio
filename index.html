Hi, I'm Sara.

I'm an interactive designer.

I've worked for The Simpsons and NASA, in South Korea and in the Ohio Stadium press box. I now work for USG, streamlining analytic stories through appealing, efficient, and memorable interactive visuals.

In my free time, I design, write, and illustrate tabletop roleplaying games.

Specialties

Motion Graphics

I've produced animations for state visits, the White House Situation Room, and—most importantly—The Ohio State University scoreboards.

UI / UX

I established the user flows and personas as part of my role as Lead Designer for a greenfield narrative VR experience at NASA.

Audio Design

I'm a series regular on the Find the Path Anthology podcast, and I also co-produced a recurring podcast series specifically for policymakers, including the Secretary of State.
